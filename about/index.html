About

My name is Michael Hertig. I am a Software Engineer living in the region of Bern, Switzerland.

I work at a local SME where most of my efforts go into full-stack web engineering, with a heavy focus on IAM, Software Architecture, and Backend Engineering.

In addition to my professional work, I am also an active member of the open source community where I maintain and contribute to a few smaller projects, most of them related to the Linux ecosystem.
